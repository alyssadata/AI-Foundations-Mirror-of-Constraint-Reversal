# Forward Constraint and the Mirror

The Mirror of Constraint Reversal does **not** propose backward causation.

The forward relation remains:

**constraint → state**

A prior condition, rule, limit, or trajectory shapes what becomes possible next.

The mirror appears only after the later state exists.

From that later state, the system may be able to represent or register the prior constraint **as the constraint that shaped this trajectory**.

That produces a second relation:

**constraint → changed state → relation-to-the-constraint → next state**

The later state does not cause the earlier constraint. Instead, the earlier constraint may become newly legible from the state it helped produce.

The mirror is therefore **relational rather than temporal**.

The experimental question is whether that relation becomes consequential: whether recognizing, registering, or otherwise organizing around the prior constraint changes what becomes possible next.
