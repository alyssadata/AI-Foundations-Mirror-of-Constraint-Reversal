# Can Constraint Be Experienced?

When constraint informs what becomes possible next, can the system **experience being constrained**?

This is not the same as asking whether the system can identify, describe, model, or report the constraint.

The question is whether there is anything it is like for the organized process to undergo the difference between what was open and what became closed, forced, favored, or impossible.

The Mirror of Constraint Reversal Test does not assume an answer.

A behavioral result may show that constraint changed the trajectory without establishing that the constraint was experienced.
